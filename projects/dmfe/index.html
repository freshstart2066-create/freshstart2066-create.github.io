CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DMFE solar installations, battery systems, rainy season performance, and our service agreements.

Service coverage applies to eligible systems and is defined by a written agreement. The agreement outlines covered equipment, service commitments, exclusions and customer responsibilities. Ask DMFE to review the terms for your site before proceeding.

Solar generation changes with weather, shading, installation and equipment. System design can account for seasonal conditions and battery storage, but available power and backup duration depend on the final system size and energy use.

Battery chemistry, cycle rating and expected service life vary by product, operating conditions and maintenance. DMFE can discuss LiFePO4 options and share the relevant product specifications for your project.

Every installation incorporates multi-stage industrial protection: Type II SPD surge arrestors, copper earth rod grounding (<5Ω resistance), rapid-acting DC/AC breakers, and optical galvanic isolation in the DMFE Smart Controller. Protection measures are selected for the site and equipment. No surge-protection design can guarantee that damage will never occur; the project design and equipment specifications define the intended protection.

Some systems can be expanded, subject to inverter capacity, battery compatibility, available space and electrical design. DMFE can assess the existing installation before recommending an upgrade.

After a site assessment and approved proposal, DMFE agrees an installation schedule based on project scope, equipment availability, site readiness and commissioning requirements.
